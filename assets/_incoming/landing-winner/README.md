# Landing winner: The Harvest Floor (reference only)

This is the winning prototype of the landing contest (`shadowrunner-root-cellar-r4` A/1, chosen
2026-10-31). Spec `landing` ports it into `apps/web`. The app never imports this folder.

- Open `index.html` from disk. It loads Malakor's rig from `../malakor-rig/`.
- `NOTES.md`: the designer's philosophy and round-4 notes.
- `screenshots/`: the acceptance look for every channel and for the 390 px phone layout.
- `stills-source/` and `PROMPTS.md`: the generated stills and their prompts.
